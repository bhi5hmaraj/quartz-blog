Hi Stranger,

I'm a huge fan of Paul Graham's idea of keeping one's identity small, so this blog will remain a random walk through topics that pique my interest. That said, the paths I've been walking have started to converge on a single, critical landscape: navigating our future with advanced AI.

My journey started with an introspection into human cognition and its pitfalls, inspired by works like Thinking, Fast and Slow. This led me to the rationality and Effective Altruism (EA) communities, which provided powerful tools for thinking about complex systems and high-stakes problems. My background in CS grounds this in technical reality.

Recently, these threads have woven together to focus on what I see as the most pressing near-term challenge: the risk of "Gradual Disempowerment" (GDR). This is the subtle erosion of human agency as AI reshapes our economic and political power structures. My explorations will grapple with this problem and architect potential solutions, like open, empowering infrastructure (OAEA) and specialized tools for robust governance (Dialectic).

So, while you'll still find posts on CS and human flourishing, they will increasingly be viewed through this lens: how do we build the systems, and become the people, capable of steering a world with powerful AI toward beneficial outcomes?
